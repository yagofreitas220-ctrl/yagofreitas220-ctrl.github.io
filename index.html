QuasiClips

Internal editing and publishing tool for our own gaming channels.

What it is

QuasiClips is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Quasi Clips, MakingClips and Viral Vault509. It helps us edit short vertical highlight videos (YouTube Shorts) about the game GTA 6.

How it works

  • The tool helps the channel owner find interesting moments in gaming live streams.
  • Each moment is edited into a vertical short: reframed to 9:16, with subtitles, a title text on screen and commentary.
  • Every video description credits the original streamer and links to the original live stream.
  • The finished short is uploaded to our own channels through the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload). The youtube.readonly scope is used only to confirm which of our channels is connected.

Who can use it

QuasiClips is not a public service. It has no sign-up and no external users. It is only used by the channel owner, on their own computer, with their own Google accounts.
