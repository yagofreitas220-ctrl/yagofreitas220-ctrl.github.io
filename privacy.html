Privacy Policy – QuasiClips

Last updated: November 18, 2026

QuasiClips ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Quasi Clips, MakingClips and Viral Vault509 to upload edited short videos to those channels.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload): used only to upload videos made by the channel owner to the channel owner's own channels.
  • YouTube read-only permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly): used only to read the name of the connected channel, so the owner can confirm the right channel was connected.

How the information is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data of anyone.
  • The app does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers or analytics.
  • The app has no external users, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access from the computer.
